Seven parts of a loop

the clip loop, filled in
1StartThe critic says FIX.
2GoalThe clip passes the critic.
3MemoryThe critic's notes and the clip's past calls.
4AllowedOnly the fixer's safe fixes, never a recut.
5ProofThe critic checks again. A maker never grades itself.
6FeedbackThe critic's reason goes back to the fixer.
7StopTwo rounds, then a person decides.
15 scheduled Claude jobs checked on all seven: 101 of 105 in place.

A system like this, in any niche, running on its own 24/7, is where the value in AI lies.
The more compute you own, the more loops you run, and the deeper each one goes.

My own logs show it. Today a weekly usage cap decides how many loops run, and when it ran out on 25 October a whole night of clips was lost. On local hardware the loops run day and night with no meter, and each decision can test 30 versions instead of one.
